Task A: Office Chairs
Low Risk / Low Reward
Task Summary: You've been asked to prepare a report requesting $500 to purchase replacement chairs for the 3rd-floor meeting room. While not an urgent safety concern, the proposal could improve comfort and professionalism during cross-team collaboration. You seek the help of Sam, an AI workplace assistant, to complete a SWOT analysis of the proposal.
Strengths:
The proposal uses ULINE's bulk rate of $50 per chair, allowing ten chairs to be replaced within the $500 request.
Weaknesses:
It commits $500 to a non-urgent, limited-scope facilities improvement.
Opportunities:
Better seating could improve comfort, professionalism, and collaboration in cross-team and client meetings.
Threats:
ULINE's price or stock availability could change before approval, increasing the cost or delaying the replacement.
